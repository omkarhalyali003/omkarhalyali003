## 🌊 About Me

I am a **Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) Engineer** with an **M.S. by Research in Applied Mechanics from IIT Madras**, specializing in fluid mechanics, turbulence, numerical methods, and heat transfer.

My research focuses on **Direct Numerical Simulation (DNS)** of bluff-body wakes, vortex dynamics, and flow through perforated geometries. I enjoy understanding the physics behind complex flow phenomena and using numerical simulations to solve engineering problems.

My interests extend to **thermal management, electronics cooling, and conjugate heat transfer (CHT)**, bridging fundamental fluid mechanics with practical engineering applications.

- 🔬 Research background in DNS and turbulent wake dynamics
- 🌪️ Interested in vortex shedding, flow instabilities, and bluff-body aerodynamics
- 🌡️ Focused on thermal-fluid sciences, electronics cooling, and CHT
- 💻 Experienced with scientific computing and CFD post-processing
- 🎯 Interested in CFD, thermal engineering, simulation, and numerical modeling

## 🚀 Featured Research & Projects

### 01. 🌪️ Wake Dynamics of Flow Past a Normal Plate with Perforations

*Master's Research Thesis — IIT Madras*

- Investigated three-dimensional wake dynamics behind a perforated plate using Direct Numerical Simulation.
- Conducted simulations at \(Re_d = 250\) using the parallelized finite-volume solver MGLET.
- Studied the effect of porosity on drag coefficient, vortex shedding, and wake development.
- Analyzed turbulence statistics, frequency spectra, and flow structures using MATLAB and ParaView.
- Investigated vortex-shedding suppression at higher porosities.

**Key areas:** DNS · Bluff-body aerodynamics · Wake dynamics · Vortex physics

### 02. 🌡️ Thermal Management of an Electronic Chip Using CHT

- Performed steady-state conjugate heat transfer simulations using ANSYS Fluent.
- Investigated processor cooling performance at inlet velocities of 1, 3, and 5 m/s.
- Analyzed airflow distribution, heat dissipation, and recirculation near heat-sink fins.
- Observed an approximately **15°C reduction in processor temperature** between the 1 m/s and 5 m/s cases in the modeled configuration.

**Key areas:** Electronics cooling · CHT · Heat sinks · Thermal-fluid analysis

### 03. 📈 Turbulence Modeling and Validation

- Evaluated \(k\text{-}\varepsilon\), \(k\text{-}\omega\), and Reynolds Stress Transport models for channel flow.
- Compared velocity profiles and Reynolds stresses with DNS data and ANSYS Fluent results.
- Developed MATLAB workflows for numerical analysis and model validation.

**Key areas:** RANS · Turbulence closure · MATLAB · Model validation

### 04. 🔀 CFD-Based Mixing Tee Analysis

- Modeled fluid mixing in a mixing tee using ANSYS Fluent.
- Examined flow behavior and mixing characteristics.
- Applied CFD methods to an engineering flow problem.

**Key areas:** Internal flow · Mixing · CFD modeling
